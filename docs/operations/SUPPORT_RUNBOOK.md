# Support runbook

Authenticate the requester and use the case/job/request link; never request passwords, full payment credentials, raw identity documents, or exact addresses in free text. Keep internal notes separate from user-visible messages.

Triage topics: access/auth, request/offer, provider conduct, job/change order, cancellation, completion, privacy/export/deletion, payment/settlement, safety, and abuse. Safety or trapped-person/fire/gas/electricity reports receive emergency guidance and immediate escalation; staff do not diagnose emergencies.

Cancellation records preserve lifecycle state and await a decision. Disputes create a financial hold and append events. Support agents may investigate and communicate but finance decisions stay with finance roles and provider verification stays with reviewers. Resolve with a reason, evidence references, customer-visible explanation, and follow-up. Export/deletion status is never sent to an unverified email.
