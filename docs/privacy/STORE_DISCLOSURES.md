# Store disclosure source

Draft disclosures cover account/contact identifiers, approximate and precise location, user content/photos/audio, diagnostics, app interactions/device identifiers, messages/support, provider documents for provider accounts, and purchase/payment metadata for physical services. Data is used for app functionality, fraud/security, support, personalization/translation, analytics when enabled, and legal obligations.

The app does not sell data in the engineering design, request background location, collect raw card credentials, or publish AI drafts automatically. Deletion is available in-app and on the public site. Exact answers must be reconciled with actual production SDKs, AI/Supabase/notification/payment subprocessors, analytics configuration, retention policy, and counsel before store submission.
