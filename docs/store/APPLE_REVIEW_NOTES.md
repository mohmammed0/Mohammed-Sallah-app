# Apple review notes draft

SALLAH coordinates Saudi customers with human-reviewed service providers for physical maintenance/services. It supports customer and provider modes in one app. Primary category: Lifestyle; secondary: Utilities/Business as appropriate after review. Suggested age rating: 4+ subject to final questionnaire; user content/messaging and safety guidance must be disclosed.

Permissions are contextual: foreground location selects service area/address and optional temporary en-route sharing; microphone records a problem description only after a tap; Photos attaches request/provider/completion evidence; notifications report marketplace/job/support events. No background location is requested. AI creates a clearly labeled, editable diagnostic draft and safety flags; only the customer can publish.

Physical-service payment defaults to offline/post-service. The app does not sell digital content or claim Apple payment processing. Account deletion is in Account > Privacy and also on the public website; recent authentication may be required. Privacy/terms/support URLs and a real reviewer customer/provider journey are human inputs. No fabricated reviewer credentials are included.
