# Apple review notes draft

SALLAH coordinates customers with human-reviewed providers for physical maintenance and home services in configured Saudi service areas. Customer and provider roles are in one app. Proposed primary category: Lifestyle, subject to the release owner's selection. Complete the current age-rating questionnaire from actual messaging, user content, AI and safety behavior; no age rating is preapproved here.

Permissions are contextual: foreground location selects a service address and supports optional provider updates to the selected job's customer for up to 30 minutes while the app remains active. The provider can stop sharing. Microphone records a problem description after a tap; camera/photo picker attaches selected request, chat, provider-verification or completion content. Notifications can be declined. Background audio/location are disabled. Confirm the submitted native manifest and device behavior before copying these notes into the console.

Before diagnostic text, selected photos or audio are transferred, the request screen names OpenAI and asks for affirmative permission. Permission applies to the current intake session and can be withdrawn for future AI transfers. Restored unsent drafts require permission again; there is a manual text-entry alternative. AI produces an editable suggestion; only the customer publishes. “Report an AI suggestion” opens in-app support without automatically attaching conversation, media or location. [Apple's current data-use rules cover third-party AI permission](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#data-use-and-sharing).

Privacy, terms and community policies are readable from sign-in and Account. Required acceptance uses the approved current document IDs and hashes; missing approved documents prevent product entry while Account and Support remain available. Approved legal text, publication and production policy activation remain owner inputs. Existing draft hashes are not accepted policies.

Online payments are disabled in this release; physical-service payment follows the disclosed offline arrangement. The app does not sell digital content or claim Apple payment processing. Account deletion is in Account > Privacy: enter the current password, start deletion, then confirm the destructive action. A separate public deletion request flow also exists. Verify delivery, identity checks, completion and lawful retention against the submitted backend.

Use the verified synthetic customer/provider journeys and a disposable deletion account. Real URLs, working contacts, reviewer credentials, native checks and store-console answers remain human inputs; none are fabricated here.
