# Google Play notes draft

Short description: اطلب خدمات الصيانة، قارن عروضًا خاصة، وتابع العمل بأمان.

Full description: SALLAH is an Arabic-first Saudi marketplace for describing a service problem by text, voice, photo and foreground location; reviewing an AI-assisted editable draft; receiving sealed offers from verified providers; selecting one provider; messaging; managing schedule, change orders, completion, rating, support and disputes. Provider accounts submit private verification documents and receive only eligible approximate-location briefs.

App category: House & Home or Lifestyle, subject to final product/legal review. Permissions: approximate/precise foreground location, microphone, photos/media, notifications. No background location, contacts, call logs, or raw payment-card access. Physical-service payment is offline by default. Data Safety answers remain drafts until production SDKs/providers are frozen. Account deletion exists in-app and on the web. Real package ID, signing, Play account, URLs, and review accounts are required.
