# Asset and license rules

- Prefer existing Sallah assets, Expo primitives, and the maintained icon library.
- New code/assets require a documented permissive license (MIT, Apache-2.0,
  BSD, ISC, OFL, or approved equivalent).
- Do not copy commercial marketplace designs, restaurant branding, logos,
  screenshots, proprietary kits, nulled templates, or unclear-license assets.
- Record dependencies in `oss-inventory.json` and notices when required.
- Optimize images and fonts; never commit raw design exports, APKs, videos, or
  private screenshots without an explicit repository purpose.
- Figma/Canva outputs remain external until their license, attribution, size,
  accessibility, and runtime need are reviewed.

The reference audit is in `docs/design/UI_REFERENCE_AUDIT.md`.
