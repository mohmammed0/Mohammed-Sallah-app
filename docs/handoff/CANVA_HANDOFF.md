# Canva handoff

Canva owns approved brand presentations, beta instructions, social assets,
advertisements, pitch visuals, store promotional images, and launch materials.

Inputs: approved Figma brand kit, product claims from current status/release
notes, licensed screenshots/assets, and legal-approved copy. Outputs remain
external until reviewed. Do not use real customer data or credentials, imply
Production/store/device validation, edit product code, or import unlicensed
logos/templates. Record asset source, license, language, dimensions, and
approval owner for every deliverable.
