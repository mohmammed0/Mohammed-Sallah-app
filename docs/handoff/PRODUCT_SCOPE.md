# Product scope

Sallah is an Arabic-first Saudi service marketplace connecting customers with
verified providers through structured diagnosis, sealed offers, protected
messaging/media, controlled location sharing, completion evidence, disputes,
ratings, and operations review.

## In the current closed-beta scope

- Customer authentication, request creation, AI-assisted text/image/voice
  intake, location/timing, offers, selection, messaging, job completion,
  disputes, rating, support, export, and deletion.
- Provider onboarding, document and service review, service areas,
  availability, matching, translated briefs, offers, jobs, evidence, earnings
  visibility, support, and account controls.
- Operations provider review, moderation, support, disputes, privacy workflows,
  audit summaries, health categories, and dead-letter visibility.
- Server-side OpenAI adapters, protected media scanning, push infrastructure,
  foreground location, and Preview build configuration.

## Explicitly out of scope

Online payments, automated payouts, background location, public production
activation, public store submission, and unapproved paid infrastructure are
not part of this handoff.
