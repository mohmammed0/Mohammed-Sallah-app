# Figma handoff

## Audience and positioning

Sallah serves Saudi customers, verified service providers, and marketplace
operations. The positioning is a trustworthy, calm, Arabic-native service
marketplace—not a restaurant clone, crypto dashboard, or payment product.

## Deliverables

- Editable variables for the design-token contract.
- Reusable components and variants for every UI state.
- Arabic-first customer, provider, and admin screens with en/ur/hi behavior.
- Clickable priority prototypes for request creation and provider operations.
- Responsive mobile and desktop specifications, focus order, reduced motion,
  touch targets, contrast, and text scaling.

Figma may explore final color, type, icon, spacing, and brand direction, but may
not alter routes, roles, data exposure, authorization, status transitions,
scanner/media rules, exact-location timing, or business scope. Use only licensed
assets and record attribution.
